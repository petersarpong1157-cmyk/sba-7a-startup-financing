# Figures

Version 2 of the analysis uses the following figures in the revised working paper.

## Figure 1. Startup median approved loan amount over time

The revised figure distinguishes nominal approved amounts from CPI-U inflation-adjusted amounts in 2025 dollars. The startup median rises from $101,200 in FY2010 to $275,000 in FY2025 in nominal terms. In 2025 dollars, the corresponding values are approximately $149,414 and $275,000.

## Figure 2. Startup share of the analytical sample

The startup share is calculated within the approved-loan analytical sample by fiscal year.

## Figure 3. Estimated startup-established loan-amount differences by industry

Points are industry-specific startup coefficients from the fully adjusted loan-amount specification with fiscal-year, project-jurisdiction, industry, and lender fixed effects and controls for processing method, business type, and collateral status. Coefficients are transformed as 100 * [exp(beta) - 1].

The corrected joint heterogeneity test is **Wald F(23, 2611) = 16.00, p < .001**, with standard errors clustered by lender (BankName). Individual industry estimates are presented descriptively; the joint test is the primary inference on industry heterogeneity.

The figure files used in the manuscript are generated from the R analysis and should be regenerated from the code when reproducing the study.
